THE WORKFLOW

From paper to payout, in three steps.

01 / CAPTURE
▧

Bring receipts in

Scan with the camera or add photos from your gallery. Remi keeps the source image alongside the corrected version.

02 / REVIEW
✓

Verify the details

On-device OCR suggests totals, tax, dates, and merchants. You confirm the fields before anything is exported.

03 / EXPORT
↗

Share one claim

Pack legible receipts onto A4 pages, add a cover sheet, and share a PDF plus a finance-friendly CSV.

MADE TO BE CHECKED

The total is yours to approve.

Remi treats extracted values as suggestions. It highlights details that need attention and blocks export until every receipt has been confirmed.
